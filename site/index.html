Visualizer

Play a cover back with no dropped notes.

  • Plays on several CPU threads, so dense covers and particle accelerators stay smooth.
  • Seek, restart and pause from the playback bar at the bottom of the window.
  • Open any .tdw or .🗿 file, or start one from the command line with -i cover.tdw.

Editor

Build a cover track by track and export it.

Open your existing covers as a project, or start from scratch. Keyboard shortcuts

Converter

Turn a sequence into a WAV file.

  • Exports match the website's sound one to one, panning included.
  • Only your RAM and your patience limit how long a cover can be.
  • Comes with a command-line version for batch jobs:
ThirtyDollarConverter.CLI -i cover.tdw -s 48000
